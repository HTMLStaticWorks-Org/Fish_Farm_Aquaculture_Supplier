FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Farm inquiries & support helpdesk.

Quick answers to common questions about on-site testing, feed delivery turnaround, and emergency hotline coverage.

How soon can an aquaculture specialist visit my pond? +
For scheduled biosecurity or aeration audits, our field team arrives within 48 to 72 hours across Tamil Nadu, Andhra Pradesh, and Kerala. For emergency water collapse consultations, remote video and lab dispatch occurs within 3 hours.
What is the minimum order quantity for commercial floating feed dispatches? +
We dispatch orders starting from 500 kg (10 bags of 50kg) up to full 15-ton truckloads. Farm-gate delivery includes moisture-barrier palletizing and batch lab certificates of analysis.
Do your paddlewheel aerators include factory warranties and spare parts? +
Yes, every aerator motor and stainless steel drive axle carries a 24-month manufacturer warranty. High-wear replacement impellers, nylon bearings, and float boats are stocked locally in our three regional hubs.
How do I submit water samples for spectrophotometric laboratory analysis? +
You can ship insulated 500ml pond water bottles to our Guindy, Chennai laboratory or drop them at our Vijayawada and Thrissur hubs. Reports for ammonia, nitrite, alkalinity, and hardness are delivered via WhatsApp within 24 hours.
